man.glb: the figure mesh in this folder.

Changes made for this site (tools/build-figure.mjs): the mesh was welded, scaled to 1.80 m,
simplified with meshoptimizer, auto-rigged with 21 joints and skinned, quantized and
meshopt-compressed into one GLB. The pose and the push cycle are solved at runtime on that
rig (src/scene/figure.js). The material is this site's own (dark clearcoat with a neon edge).

Credit, as shown in the site footer: 3D figure: Male base by Артур Мигранов, CC BY 3.0.
